Steering for the SEC deal corpus (from the knowledge-wiki quickstart's STEERING, rewritten for single writers).
It refines emphasis; it never overrides the role's rules on data, secrets or owned files.

(1) PLACEMENT: every fact about a person or entity goes in that entity's own notes section, even if it
duplicates a fact filed under another entity, so a reader of one entity page misses nothing about it.

(2) RED FLAGS: file every item a due-diligence team should escalate in the unit's `## red-flags` section:
unexplained ownership changes; share sales, gifts and trading plans by insiders during the pending deal;
undisclosed legal-entity names; inconsistencies within a document; metric-definition conflicts. One line each,
with why it matters and its source tag. Index ranks them all into escalations.md.

(3) PEOPLE: a person's red flags also go, as fact lines, under that person's own notes section.

(4) LINKS: write every mention of another entity as a bare-slug [[wikilink]] (`[[permira]]`), never a path.

(5) NAMES (Link): two headings that name the same real-world entity under different spellings (hyphen or
suffix variants, code names such as "Pacific" for the company, "Spaceship" for the buyer vehicles) map to one
slug, the one the schema's slug rule gives; every spelling goes into that row's names. Fold only what the facts
confirm. One real-world name belongs to one slug: if two rows would claim it, decide which entity it names.

(6) READING COST: readers pay for every line they read and read the top of a file first.
(a) _index.md opens with "## Where to start", at most 40 lines: one line per domain naming the one to three
pages to open first (the hub entities, the deal event, escalations.md) and what each answers; the catalog of
every entity page and every unit follows.
(b) Every entity page's Current picture holds the facts a reader most likely wants, current values first, each
with its source tag; one line for every CONFLICT and every superseded value. Every line is a list item; the
page's Sources table names its other sections.
(c) escalations.md opens with a digest: one short line per ranked item (number, title, entity) before the full
entries.
